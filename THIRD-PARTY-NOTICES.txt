VLC plugins for DVD playback (shipped in libvlc\win-x64\plugins)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  access\libdvdnav_plugin.dll
  access\libdvdread_plugin.dll
  packetizer\libpacketizer_a52_plugin.dll
License:  GNU General Public License, version 2 or later (licenses\GPL-2.0.txt)
Origin:   VLC media player 3.0.24, copied unchanged from a VLC installation. https://www.videolan.org/vlc/
Source:   https://code.videolan.org/videolan/vlc (tag 3.0.24); the DVD reading libraries are libdvdread and libdvdnav,
          https://code.videolan.org/videolan
          The source of these three files, exactly as in VLC 3.0.24, can also be had on request: open an issue at
          https://github.com/SweWolf/SwewolfMediaPlayer
These plugins are separate files that LibVLC loads when a DVD is played. They are not part of the program's own code.
Decryption of copy-protected discs (libdvdcss) is not included.
